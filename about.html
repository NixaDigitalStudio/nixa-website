Our Story

A family kitchen, a brick oven, and a love of Italian cooking, all brought home to Nixa.

How It Began

From a nonna's kitchen to the Ozarks

Olive & Hearth is the dream of the Rinaldi family. Their recipes crossed an ocean, came through three generations, and finally ended up in a little brick dining room in Nixa, Missouri.

Our grandmother Rosa cooked for anyone who walked through her door. Sunday lunch at her table often lasted until dinner: fresh pasta drying over the backs of chairs, a pot of ragù on the stove since sunrise, and a tiramisu waiting in the icebox.

When we moved to southwest Missouri, we missed that table. So we built one big enough for the whole town. We kept Rosa's recipes and her habit of making everything from scratch, and we added a wood-fired oven, because some things are worth the smoke.

Our Cooking

Italian-inspired, Ozarks-grown

We cook in the spirit of Italy's regional kitchens: simple dishes, very good ingredients, and patience.

48-hour dough

Our pizza dough uses Italian “00” flour and a slow, cold rise for a light, airy crust with real flavor.

Sauces with time

The Sunday ragù simmers for six hours. The marinara uses San Marzano tomatoes, garlic, basil and nothing else.

Neighbors first

We buy from local farms, bakeries and creameries whenever we can, and we change the menu when the seasons change.

The Atmosphere

Warm, unhurried and a little bit loud

Our dining room was designed to feel like an old trattoria. It has exposed brick, terracotta floors, deep green banquettes, linen-covered tables and the steady glow of the oven at the far end of the room.

Come in jeans or come dressed up. Bring the kids, bring your parents, bring a first date. Stay as long as you like. Nobody here will rush you through dessert.

Inside the Kitchen

A day at Olive & Hearth

  1. 7:00 AM: The fire is lit

    The oven needs nearly three hours of Missouri oak to reach full heat.

  2. 9:00 AM: Pasta day begins

    Six pastas are mixed, rolled and cut by hand for the evening's service.

  3. 1:00 PM: Desserts are made

    Tiramisù is layered, panna cotta is set, and cannoli shells are fried.

  4. 4:00 PM: Doors open

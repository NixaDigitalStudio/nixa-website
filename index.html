About Us

Locally Owned. Built on Honest Work.

Ozark Small Engine & E-Bike Repair is a small, locally run shop right here in Nixa, Missouri. We started it for a simple reason: folks around here deserve a repair shop that picks up the phone, tells them the truth, and fixes things right the first time.

Your mower is how you take care of your yard. Your trimmer keeps the fence line clean, and your e-bike might be how you get around town. When one of them breaks, you don't need a runaround. You need someone who will take a careful look, explain what's going on, and give you a fair price.

We take pride in every repair that leaves our bench, big or small. If something isn't worth fixing, we'll tell you that too. Our goal is to keep our neighbors' equipment running season after season.

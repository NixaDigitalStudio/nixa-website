Benvenuti

An Italian kitchen with an Ozarks heart

Olive & Hearth started with a simple idea: cook the way Italian families cook at home, and share it with our neighbors in Nixa.

Our dough rests for 48 hours before it meets the fire. Our pasta is rolled and cut by hand each morning. Our sauces simmer low and slow, and many of our vegetables come from farms a short drive down the road. Nothing here is complicated. It's just cooked with care.

The Olive & Hearth Way

Three things we never rush

Handmade pasta

Semolina, farm eggs and a rolling pin. Six pastas, cut fresh every morning in our open kitchen.

The wood-fired oven

Our brick oven burns Missouri oak at 850°. A pizza bakes in about 90 seconds, blistered and smoky.

Local & seasonal

Tomatoes, greens and herbs from Ozarks growers when they're at their best. The menu follows the seasons.

The Dining Room

Brick, candlelight and the glow of the oven

Settle into a green leather banquette, order a bottle of something Italian, and watch the pizzaiolo work the fire. It's a room for date nights, birthday dinners and long Sunday lunches with the whole family.

Our back room seats up to 24 guests for private celebrations.
